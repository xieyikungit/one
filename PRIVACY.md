# 有迹隐私政策 / Youji Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-10-21

## 中文

有迹帮助你记录人生经历、目标、等级和徽章。当前版本无需账号，不使用开发者服务器，也没有广告或第三方分析 SDK。

- **本机数据**：你填写的经历、备注、人生档案、目标和章节标题保存在设备本机，可随系统设备备份保存。Apple 健康每日步数单独缓存在本机，并排除在系统设备备份之外。你也可以在「我的人生 → 数据」导出 JSON 备份。
- **Apple 健康**：仅在你主动授权后读取步数，用于运动经验、徽章和回顾。应用不写入 Apple 健康，也不向开发者服务器发送步数。JSON 备份和 CSV 导出均不包含 Apple 健康步数；设备更换后可重新授权同步。
- **地点**：你填写城市时，应用可能通过 Apple 地图服务查询城市坐标，用于显示地图标记。应用不请求或读取你的实时定位。
- **导出与分享**：你主动导出的 JSON 包含手动记录、目标和档案，CSV 包含经历记录。文件可能含你填写的敏感信息；保存位置和分享对象由你选择。
- **删除**：在「我的人生 → 数据 → 隐私与数据控制」可清除设备内的经历、档案、目标、健康步数缓存和导入前快照。此前导出到其他位置的文件需由你自行删除。Apple 健康中的原始数据不受影响。

如有隐私问题，可通过 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/xieyikungit/one/issues) 联系项目维护者。政策随功能变化更新。

## English

Youji helps you record life experiences, goals, levels, and badges. The current version requires no account, uses no developer server, and includes no ads or third-party analytics SDKs.

- **On-device data:** Experiences, notes, profile details, goals, and chapter titles are stored on your device and may be included in system device backups. Daily step counts from Apple Health are cached separately on your device and excluded from system device backups. You can also export a JSON backup from Profile → Data.
- **Apple Health:** The app reads step counts only after you authorize access, to calculate exercise XP, badges, and recaps. It does not write to Apple Health or send steps to a developer server. JSON backups and CSV exports do not include Apple Health steps; you can authorize syncing again after restoring.
- **Places:** When you enter a city, the app may use Apple Maps to resolve its coordinates for a map marker. It does not request or read your live location.
- **Exports:** JSON files you choose to export contain manual records, goals, and profile details. CSV files contain experiences. These files may include sensitive information you entered; you choose their destination and recipients.
- **Deletion:** Profile → Data → Privacy and data controls lets you remove experiences, profile details, goals, cached health steps, and pre-import snapshots from this device. You must separately delete files exported elsewhere. Source data in Apple Health is unaffected.

For privacy questions, contact the maintainers through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/xieyikungit/one/issues). This policy will be updated as the app changes.
